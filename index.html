Hello, World

By day, I'm a software engineer, so it only felt right to start this the way every new project begins.

In between, I kept searching for things I'd love to wear or keep, and came up empty every time. Nothing felt like me. So I thought: why not make them myself?

That's how Ellie & Pip was born. Ellie and Pip are a reminder that this started small, a little scary, but full of love. Every design here is something I wished existed. For me, and now for you.

— Sofy
